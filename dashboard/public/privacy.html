Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Applies to the TokenTracker CLI, macOS app, Windows app, Linux app, and website.

TokenTracker is local-first. It reads local AI coding tool logs, extracts usage metrics, and stores the resulting history on your device. The local dashboard, parsers, and database run on your machine.

1. Data that stays local

  • Prompts, responses, conversation bodies, source code, project files, file contents, and provider credentials are not collected or uploaded by the tracker.
  • Usage buckets, project attribution, session records, preferences, caches, and imported data are stored in your local TokenTracker data directory.
  • Delete the TokenTracker data directory to remove the local history. Uninstalling also removes hooks installed into supported tools.

2. Network requests

The tracker may contact provider APIs when you enable a provider quota or usage reader. Those requests go directly from your machine to the provider and use credentials already stored by that provider. TokenTracker does not retain the credentials or provider response bodies.

The dashboard may contact GitHub for public repository metadata, a model-pricing source for pricing updates, PostHog for optional product analytics, and provider status or exchange-rate services when you open the related feature. You can disable telemetry with TOKENTRACKER_NO_TELEMETRY=1, DO_NOT_TRACK=1, or the telemetry setting in your local configuration.

3. Sign-in and OAuth

The public website may use InsForge authentication and OAuth for account-protected routes. Authentication data is sent to the selected authentication service when you sign in. Local usage history is not uploaded for a cross-device account view, public profile, or leaderboard.

4. Sharing and exports

Import and export are local file operations. A file leaves your device only when you choose to share it through another application or service. Review an export before sharing it.

5. Third parties

Third-party requests are made only for the feature that needs them. Their privacy policies govern data they receive directly from your browser or device. TokenTracker does not sell or rent your data.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests related to an account used for sign-in, contact rynnsun0509@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, provider credentials, prompts, or source code.
