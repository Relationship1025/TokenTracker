TokenTracker

TokenTracker local features

TokenTracker is a local-first, open-source usage tracker. There are no TokenTracker Cloud subscriptions or paid hosted plans.

Included locally

Token collection, supported provider integrations, cost estimates, provider limits, charts, project and session views, desktop features, and local import/export are available without payment or an account.

Data and accounts

Your usage history is processed by the local dashboard and stored on your machine. Sign-in and OAuth remain available where the site needs account protection, but signing in does not enable a paid plan, cross-device usage view, public profile, or leaderboard.
